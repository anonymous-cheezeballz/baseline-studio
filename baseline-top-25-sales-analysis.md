# Baseline Studio — Top 25 Sales Opportunity Analysis

**Source of truth:** `baseline-prospect-audit-clean.md` (canonical 100-business audit)
**Research method:** Browser navigation, web_extract, Google Maps, and curl only. No `web_search`.

---

## Overview

This document ranks 25 businesses most likely to become realistic Baseline Studio clients, evaluated against five sales-readiness factors:

1. **Closeability** — Owner accessibility, local ownership, simple decision-making, no corporate barriers
2. **Economic Value** — Direct consumer revenue, reasonable pricing, ability to sustain recurring spend
3. **Demonstrability** — Clear, visible marketing problem Baseline can show improvement on quickly
4. **Recurring Revenue Potential** — Ongoing content/social/branding needs
5. **Portfolio Value** — Strong before/after story for Baseline's own marketing

Businesses with high Opportunity Scores but already-polished websites (#68 Specialized Performance Rehab, #22 Energia Wellness) were deprioritized — closeability and demonstrable need are low when marketing is already competent.
